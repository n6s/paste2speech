About Paste to Speech

Paste to Speech is a free text-to-speech reader built by Roger Brown. After being laid off, I couldn’t justify a Speechify subscription, so I started building a reader for myself. What began out of necessity became something useful enough to share.

Paste text, choose a voice, and listen. No subscription required. If it helps you and you’d like to support the project, optional tips are welcome.

Independent project; not affiliated with Speechify. Voice generation uses Kokoro and Transformers.js; PDF extraction uses PDF.js.

Send feedback

Something not working, or a word sounding wrong? Tell me here. A reply address is optional.

Your message, optional email, and basic diagnostic details are sent: test or production site, browser family, desktop or mobile category, selected engine and voice, app release, and playback status. Device detection is approximate. Your reading, PDF filename, and audio are not attached. Feedback text and diagnostics are reviewed using OpenAI Codex to help improve the site; the optional reply-address field is not included in that review. Roger receives reviewed summaries through Telegram. Original reports and local review records are deleted after 90 days; notification copies may remain in Roger’s Telegram chat. Useful issues may be retained without contact details. Replies are not automatic.

Privacy

PDFs and on-device Kokoro speech are processed in your browser. The private Tailscale reader also offers Workstation - Kokoro, which sends reading text to your workstation for speech generation. Reading text and audio are not saved on the server. Reloading or closing the page discards them. Voice and reading preferences are saved in this browser. Kokoro downloads are cached when possible; use “Remove downloaded voices” to remove them.

Browser voices use your device’s speech provider, which may process speech online. Kokoro model downloads contact Hugging Face, which receives normal connection information. Cloudflare serves this website and receives normal connection information.

We count page views, playback starts, Kokoro readiness and failures, and preparation time to improve the reader. These are daily totals, not visitor profiles. We do not store IP addresses, reading content, filenames, or persistent visitor identifiers in these statistics. Statistics expire after 90 days. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable these events. Feedback is separate and is sent only when you submit the form.
