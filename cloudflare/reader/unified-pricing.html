PasteToSpeech pricing

Listen with free browser voices, create summaries for free, or use one shared balance for paid cloud voices and scanning text from images and PDFs.

Browser voices and summaries — free

Browser voices require no purchase. Creating summaries uses no credits and remains subject to service availability. Making paid-voice audio from a summary uses the usual speech credits.

Shared balance pack — $10 USD plus applicable tax

A one-time purchase adds 1,000 shared credits. Applicable taxes and the final total appear in Paddle checkout. No subscription, automatic renewal, automatic refill or scheduled credit expiry. Verify your email to protect and recover your balance.

Paid cloud speech uses 1 credit per 1,000 Unicode characters in cleaned reading text, counted exactly with fractional credits, including spaces and punctuation. Successful sections consume credits; failed or unstarted sections release their holds. Replay, seeking and speed changes use no more credits.

Cloud scanning uses 2 credits per submitted page when the whole file produces complete, validated output saved privately. Blank pages within an otherwise successful file count. Failed, incomplete or entirely blank results use no customer credits. Device PDF extraction and saved scan replay are free. Paid speech from scanned text is a separate action that uses additional credits.

A full pack is approximately 18.5 hours of listening or 500 scanned pages, or a mix of both. Listening assumes 150 words per minute and six characters per word, including spaces and punctuation; duration varies. These are alternative uses of one balance, not separate allowances. Exact credits and holds are shown in Balance & usage.

Cloud scanning accepts supported unencrypted PDFs up to 10 pages or PNG/JPEG/WebP images, up to 6 MB, after explicit whole-file Mistral upload consent. Saved scan results and prepared recordings expire after 24 hours. Download them before expiry. Reprocessing the same expired scan is currently blocked; there are no automatic paid retries.
