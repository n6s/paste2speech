Terms, refunds and privacy

Reader · Your account

Shared credits

A one-time $10 USD pack adds 1,000 shared credits for paid cloud speech and scanning. Applicable taxes and the final total appear in Paddle checkout. There is no subscription, automatic renewal or automatic refill. Purchased credit has no scheduled expiration. Sign in with a verified email before purchasing.

Speech uses 1 credit per 1,000 Unicode characters in cleaned reading text, including spaces and punctuation. Charges count exactly: one character is 0.001 credit. Credits are held when work starts. Successfully generated sections consume their hold, including when you later cancel; failed or unstarted sections release it. Replay, seeking, speed changes and existing downloads use no more credits. Listening duration is an estimate, not an entitlement.

Cloud scanning uses 2 credits per submitted page only when the whole file produces complete, validated output saved privately. This includes blank pages within an otherwise successful file. Failed, incomplete or entirely blank results use no customer credits. Device PDF extraction and saved replay use no credits. Saved results expire after 24 hours; processing the same file again after expiry is currently blocked. There are no automatic paid retries. Download your text before expiry.

Cloud scanning accepts PNG/JPEG/WebP images or supported unencrypted PDFs up to 10 pages, no larger than 6 MB. Explicit consent sends the whole selected file to Mistral. You may optionally remember this choice for your account in this browser, so files you deliberately paste or choose are scanned automatically at 2 credits per page, within your available balance and the file/page limits. Turn off the choice under Cloud actions to ask each time. Nothing is read from your clipboard or uploaded in the background. Scanning and paid speech from the extracted text are separate actions with separate charges against the same balance. Image files count as one page; PDF page counts are verified before charging.

Free browser voices and summaries

Browser voices, local PDF extraction and replay of available prepared results use no credits. Creating summaries uses no credits and remains subject to existing availability limits. Creating paid-voice audio from a summary uses the usual speech credits.

Availability and recordings

WaveNet starts playback as audio becomes ready. Long documents are prepared automatically in parts, with one preparation at a time per account. Provider quotas, temporary availability and abuse controls may pause generation. Your credit remains on your account; no app-imposed cumulative, daily or monthly provider spending ceiling applies. Free browser voices remain available.

Recordings are available for 24 hours. Download an MP4 recording with built-in subtitles before expiry. Clear removes access to the recording and schedules deletion. Expired recordings cannot be recovered; generating them again consumes credit. Retention cleanup may finish after access expires.

Refunds and support

Request a proportional refund for unused purchased shared credit by emailing support@pastetospeech.com with your account email and Paddle receipt or transaction reference. Do not send card information or private reading text. Service failures receive appropriate refunds or restored credit after review.

Purchases are sold by Paddle and are subject to the Paddle Buyer Terms and Paddle Refund Policy. Paddle requirements and applicable consumer rights take precedence over our unused-credit policy, including any right to a full refund. Statutory withdrawal periods and digital-content consent conditions depend on your location and transaction; using credits does not automatically waive mandatory rights. Request withdrawal or a refund through the support link in your Paddle receipt or Paddle payment support; eligible transactions may also show a withdrawal option in Paddle’s customer portal.

Approved refunds, credits and chargebacks remove the proportional purchased units, capped at the original pack. Pending or rejected refunds do not remove units. A refund covering spent or held credits creates a shortfall; new paid work pauses until it is resolved, and unused holds released later reduce that shortfall. A later purchase covers any remaining shortfall first. Existing legacy purchases and in-flight work retain their original accounting. Purchases and adjustments are verified on the server; delayed confirmation does not mean you should pay again.

Privacy

PDF extraction runs on your device. Browser voices use your device’s speech service, which may connect to the internet. WaveNet sends cleaned reading text to Google Cloud for synthesis. Cloudflare privately stores prepared text and audio temporarily for playback and downloads. With your explicit OCR consent, the whole selected file is sent to Mistral; its extracted result is stored privately in Cloudflare for up to 24 hours. Clear removes the loaded document from this tab while the server result remains available until expiry. Reading text is not stored in the credit ledger or operational logs.

Email sign-in uses Resend and a single-use, 15-minute link opened in the requesting browser. The account page is permanent: request a new link there when needed. Sessions expire after 30 days. Account, payment and numeric usage records are retained to maintain credit, reconcile payments and resolve disputes. Paddle processes payment details; this site does not store your card number.

Feedback and aggregate statistics are retained for 90 days. Feedback is reviewed using OpenAI Codex; optional reply addresses are excluded from that review. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable aggregate statistics. Browser preferences, including optional versioned cloud-action choices for this account, are stored locally. Summary sends the current editor text to Cloudflare AI only when you choose to summarize. A remembered Summary choice makes that action one tap; typing and pasting text do not start inference. Creating summaries uses no credits, and the Summary service does not store source or output in its ledger or object storage. Contact support for account or privacy requests.

PasteToSpeech Chrome extension

The extension receives the plain text you select when you choose “Read with PasteToSpeech”. It passes that text to the reader at pastetospeech.com and attempts playback using your saved reader settings. It does not scan source webpages in the background or collect their URLs or your browsing history. Selected text may contain personal or sensitive information; send only text you intend the reader and your chosen speech service to process.

One pending selection and delivery metadata are held in Chrome’s extension session memory. The pending text is removed after the reader acknowledges delivery, when you close the extension’s reader tab, or when Chrome restarts. A delivery expires after ten minutes; expired data is removed on the next extension wake or access. Pending text is not placed in URLs, synced storage, or extension logs. The reader stores only the last accepted request identifier and sequence in tab session storage to prevent duplicate automatic playback. Reader preferences and any saved reading-recovery information follow the reader’s existing local storage behavior.

After delivery, browser voices use your device’s speech service, which may connect to the internet. Google WaveNet sends cleaned reading text to Google Cloud for synthesis; Cloudflare stores prepared text and audio temporarily as described above. The extension has no separate account or payment form. Optional reader accounts, purchases, feedback, statistics, and recording retention remain covered by this page’s other provisions.

PasteToSpeech’s use of information received from the Chrome extension is limited to providing or improving its stated speech-reading features. We do not sell this information, use it for personalized advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. Our use and transfer of information received through the extension adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Contact support@pastetospeech.com for privacy questions or deletion requests.
