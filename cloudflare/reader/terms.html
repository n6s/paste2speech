Speech credit

A one-time $10 USD speech pack includes 1,000,000 characters. Applicable taxes and the final total appear in Paddle checkout. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Sign in using your verified email before purchasing. Purchased credit has no scheduled expiration.

Allowance counts Unicode characters in cleaned reading text, including spaces and punctuation, for successfully generated sections. Failed or unstarted sections release reserved credit. Successfully generated sections still consume credit when you cancel. Replay, speed changes and existing downloads are free. Speech duration is an estimate, not an entitlement.

Availability and recordings

WaveNet starts playback as audio becomes ready. Long documents are prepared automatically in parts, with one preparation at a time per account. Daily and monthly service capacity limits may temporarily pause generation and new purchases. Your credit remains on your account; the account page shows reset times. Free browser voices remain available.

Recordings are available for 24 hours. Download an MP4 recording with built-in subtitles before expiry. Clear removes access to the recording and schedules deletion. Expired recordings cannot be recovered; generating them again consumes credit. Retention cleanup may finish after access expires.

Refunds and support

Request a proportional refund for unused purchased credit by emailing rogerpbrown@gmail.com with your account email and Paddle receipt or transaction reference. Do not send card information or private reading text. Service failures receive appropriate refunds or restored credit after review. Paddle requirements and applicable consumer rights take precedence.

Approved refunds remove the associated credit. If a refund or chargeback covers credit already used, further WaveNet generation pauses until the adjustment is resolved. Purchases and refunds are verified on the server; delayed confirmation does not mean you should pay again.

Privacy

PDF extraction runs on your device. Browser voices use your device’s speech service, which may connect to the internet. WaveNet sends cleaned reading text to Google Cloud for synthesis. Cloudflare privately stores prepared text and audio temporarily for playback and downloads. Reading text is not stored in the credit ledger or operational logs.

Email sign-in uses Resend and a single-use, 15-minute link opened in the requesting browser. The account page is permanent: request a new link there when needed. Sessions expire after 30 days. Account, payment and numeric usage records are retained to maintain credit, reconcile payments and resolve disputes. Paddle processes payment details; this site does not store your card number.

Feedback and aggregate statistics are retained for 90 days. Feedback is reviewed using OpenAI Codex; optional reply addresses are excluded from that review. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable aggregate statistics. Browser preferences are stored locally. Contact support for account or privacy requests.

PasteToSpeech Chrome extension

The extension receives the plain text you select when you choose “Read with PasteToSpeech”. It passes that text to the reader at pastetospeech.com and attempts playback using your saved reader settings. It does not scan source webpages in the background or collect their URLs or your browsing history. Selected text may contain personal or sensitive information; send only text you intend the reader and your chosen speech service to process.

One pending selection and delivery metadata are held in Chrome’s extension session memory. The pending text is removed after the reader acknowledges delivery, when you close the extension’s reader tab, or when Chrome restarts. A delivery expires after ten minutes; expired data is removed on the next extension wake or access. Pending text is not placed in URLs, synced storage, or extension logs. The reader stores only the last accepted request identifier and sequence in tab session storage to prevent duplicate automatic playback. Reader preferences and any saved reading-recovery information follow the reader’s existing local storage behavior.

After delivery, browser voices use your device’s speech service, which may connect to the internet. Google WaveNet sends cleaned reading text to Google Cloud for synthesis; Cloudflare stores prepared text and audio temporarily as described above. The extension has no separate account or payment form. Optional reader accounts, purchases, feedback, statistics, and recording retention remain covered by this page’s other provisions.

PasteToSpeech’s use of information received from the Chrome extension is limited to providing or improving its stated speech-reading features. We do not sell this information, use it for personalized advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. Our use and transfer of information received through the extension adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Contact rogerpbrown@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests.
