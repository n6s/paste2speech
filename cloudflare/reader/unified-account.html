How scanning works

Optical character recognition (OCR) reads text from images. PDFs are checked on your device first; local extraction uses no credits. If cloud scanning is needed, review the whole file’s page count and credit cost before agreeing to send it to Mistral. There are no automatic paid retries. Saved results are private to your account and expire after 24 hours.

Scanning text and creating paid-voice audio are separate actions. Listening to scanned text with paid cloud voices uses additional speech credits; browser voices remain free.

Summaries

Creating summaries uses no credits. Making paid-voice audio from a summary uses the usual speech credits. Browser-voice playback is free. Summary creation remains subject to the service’s existing availability limits.
