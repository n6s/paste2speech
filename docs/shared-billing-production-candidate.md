# Shared billing production candidate — October 11, 2026

Status: local preparation approved; **no live catalog changes, migrations, deployments, payments, provider calls or legal acceptance are authorized by this preparation**. The proposed runtime and public UI are prepared in `main`. The public candidate configuration intentionally has empty shared SKU IDs and owner-first opening (`PUBLIC_LAUNCH_OPEN=0`). Deployment preflight rejects it until a separate live catalog is verified. Existing speech/OCR IDs remain for reconciliation.

## Proposed customer purchase and rates

A separate live Paddle product, **PasteToSpeech shared balance**, with a one-time **$10 USD** price, quantity one, **1,000 shared credits**, plus applicable checkout taxes. Standard digital goods tax category is the existing live catalog category; verify it for the new product. No subscription, renewal, automatic refill or scheduled credit expiry.

The ledger holds 1,000 integer units per credit. Speech consumes exactly one unit per Unicode character (one credit per 1,000 characters), including spaces and punctuation in cleaned text, on successful sections. Cloud OCR consumes 2,000 units (two credits) per submitted page only when the whole file produces complete validated output saved privately. Failed/unstarted speech and unsuccessful OCR release customer holds. Prepared replay, browser voices, device PDF extraction and saved scan replay are free. Creating summaries is free within existing availability limits; paid speech from summaries or scanned text consumes the usual speech credit.

The same pack can fund about 18.5 hours **or** 500 scanned pages, or a mixture. Listening estimate assumes 150 words/minute and six characters/word; it is not a duration entitlement or inferred listening history. Compact balance and native Details disclosures are shared between staging and public. Upload consent, whole-file cost and page count remain visible before scanning.

## Ledger migration and compatibility

Only `cloudflare/reader/migrations/0017_unified_wallet.sql` is proposed for production. **Do not apply staging migration 0010 to production.** Production controls are immutable and live-only; production rejects `test-grant`. New wallets start at zero with no copied balances, free grant or replayed purchases. Existing account IDs, identities, balances, debt, purchases, immutable grants/adjustments, usage and audit records remain in place. Existing speech and OCR reservations acquire `wallet_version=legacy`; successful/failed legacy work and later legacy refunds keep their original ledgers. Shared work is versioned `unified-v1` and cannot have its provenance rewritten.

The production runtime rejects sandbox credentials, staging database controls, fixture OCR providers, trial/local modes and reused legacy SKU IDs. New shared orders require explicit `offer=credits`: stale speech/OCR clients receive an error and must refresh, rather than unknowingly selecting a new product. Reconciliation remains routed separately for old speech, old OCR and shared orders. Existing signed webhooks feed all three queues; the existing PaymentCoordinator binding delegates the named `unified-v1` object without creating a new binding or credential.

## Current refund copy and source verification

Our additional support policy offers proportional refunds for unused purchased credit and appropriate refunds/restored credit for service failures. It explicitly defers to Paddle and mandatory consumer rights, including any applicable full refund. It links Paddle receipt/customer-portal support and does not claim that using credits automatically waives statutory rights.

Verified October 11 from [Paddle Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) (dated March 31, 2026) and [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms): statutory rights depend on location; the refund policy provides a 14-day withdrawal period in EU/EEA/Switzerland/UK with express digital-content waiver conditions, and other regional periods/rights. The highest non-waivable/additional rights apply. Requests can use receipt support/Paddle.net; eligible EU transactions can show a portal withdrawal button. These rights are not limited to unused credits. Refund entitlement must use the terms in effect for that purchase.

[Official Paddle adjustment documentation](https://developer.paddle.com/build/transactions/create-transaction-adjustments/) confirms live refunds may require Paddle approval. Pending/rejected adjustments do not remove credits. Approved proportional principal refunds remove exact integer units, capped at the original grant; reversals restore once. The [official adjustment schema](https://developer.paddle.com/api-reference/adjustments/list-adjustments/) states that Paddle marks the original adjustment `reversed` when creating a chargeback/credit reversal; tests include both that original and the separate reversal without double-restoring. Refunds against spent/held units create debt, released unused holds reduce it, and new purchases cover remaining debt first. Do not promise immediate refund approval or zero merchant cost. The historical owner refund had a $1.04 retained cost; the fee for a new transaction has not been established and no new refund is approved here.

## Pause and recovery (forward compatible)

Use the candidate-compatible Worker and schema. Set **`ENABLE_PAYMENTS=0`** to stop new purchases and **`PAID_ADMISSIONS_PAUSED=1`** to stop fresh paid speech/OCR admissions. Keep `UNIFIED_CREDITS=1`, `UNIFIED_LIVE_APPROVED=1`, `ENABLE_UNIFIED_PAYMENTS=1`, live shared and legacy SKU IDs, credentials, webhook endpoint and cron/alarms enabled. `ENABLE_LIVE_SYNTHESIS=1` and `ENABLE_DOCUMENT_OCR=1` allow previously admitted work to finish; cached results, prepared recordings, Summary creation and browser voices remain usable. Debt continues to stop unpaid dispatch as before.

**Do not use `BACKGROUND_WORK_PAUSED=1`, disable unified routing, deploy a pre-wallet Worker, drop schema, replay grants or restore a backup over later financial activity.** Those actions can strand reconciliation/settlements or change accounting provenance. Retain all records and deploy a compatible forward fix; check failed webhook/payment recovery counters and do not blindly retry purchases. An unused migration can remain in place if activation stops; reopening new work requires fresh approval/config review after recovery.
